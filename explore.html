A second view · sound over chronology

Arranged by style

The same bands, positioned by sound alone with year ignored. Every anchor here is a movement, not just a broad family - metal splits into heavy metal, thrash, death and black, nu / alt-metal, and metalcore, punk into punk, hardcore, post-hardcore, and pop-punk, and so on. Dot colors group the movements back into their parent families, a band sits at its movement, and a genre-blender drifts toward a second one.

A curated map, not a complete survey — it follows the strongest, best-documented connections, and plenty of artists could sit in two lanes at once. Album picks are chosen for a reason (a starting point, a critical peak, a fan favorite, a deep cut) rather than listed in full. Line-up notes flag significant figures and shared members. The recent-underground picks (ringed) are a snapshot that will date the fastest.
